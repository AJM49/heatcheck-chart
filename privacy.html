PRIVACY POLICY

Heat Check — The Momentum Chart  ·  Effective October 17, 2026

What this site collects

Heat Check does not ask you to create an account, sign in, or hand over your name or email address to read the charts. The site itself does not run its own analytics and does not build profiles of individual visitors.

If you use the contact form on the Contact page, the message you type (your name, email address, and message text) is sent to the site operator so your message can be answered. It is not sold, and it is not used for advertising.

Advertising and cookies

This site shows ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its third-party vendors use cookies and similar identifiers to serve ads, including:

  • Cookies for ad delivery and measurement — to show ads, count views and clicks, and detect fraud and abuse.
  • Personalised ads — Google may use your visits to this and other sites to show ads matched to your interests, including through the DoubleClick cookie.
  • Third-party vendors — other ad vendors may place and read their own cookies on your browser when an ad is served.

Google's use of advertising cookies is described in Google's advertising technology policy. You can control personalised ads from Google at adssettings.google.com, and opt out of personalised advertising from many vendors at optout.aboutads.info or optout.networkadvertising.org.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Netlify. Like most hosts, Netlify may process standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested) to deliver the site, keep it secure, and diagnose problems. Heat Check does not receive or store those logs as visitor profiles.

Children

Heat Check is a general-audience music chart. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
